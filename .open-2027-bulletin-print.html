1. Invitation

Charles Michael Sports Hub welcomes athletes, clubs, dojangs and delegations to the Mount Kenya International Taekwondo Open Tournament. The two-day event aims to support athlete development and provide a welcoming, well-run competition in Limuru, with junior and senior participation.

2. Eligibility and athlete welfare

  • Entry is intended for athletes registered through a club, dojang or team, subject to the final event regulations.
  • Each athlete must meet the published age and weight requirements and provide acceptable proof of age at registration or weigh-in.
  • Registration deadlines, entry requirements and any required federation licence or coach accreditation will be published with the entry process.
  • Athletes should be medically fit to compete. Clubs and athletes are responsible for appropriate personal accident and medical insurance.
Important: The detailed event regulations will state the final eligibility rules, age cut-off dates, required documents and any permitted movement between divisions.

Competition information

8. Rules and equipment

The tournament is planned to follow current World Taekwondo competition rules for the relevant events, subject to technical approval and any published event-specific adaptations. The final applicable rules will be communicated in the event regulations.

  • A taekwondo uniform (dobok) is required for competition.
  • For Kyorugi, athletes should be prepared with the protective equipment required by the final rules and event regulations, including approved head, mouth, trunk, forearm and shin protection, plus other required protective items.
  • Coaches and athletes must follow instructions from referees, technical officials and event staff.
  • Final equipment approval, electronic scoring requirements and Poomsae requirements will be published before the event.

9. Awards

The planned awards are medals and certificates for first, second and third place. Any team or individual special awards will be confirmed in the event regulations.

10. Protests and appeals

The authorised person, submission process, time limit and any protest fee will be stated in the event regulations. Coaches and team representatives should raise competition matters promptly through the appointed technical officials.

11. Medical, insurance and responsibility

Participation involves the ordinary risks of combat sport. Athletes should only compete when medically fit, and clubs or guardians should ensure suitable medical and accident insurance. Emergency response arrangements and participant terms will be communicated with the final event information.

The event organiser will publish applicable participation terms before athlete entries are finalised. Nothing in this bulletin replaces those terms or a participant’s own insurance arrangements.

12. Changes and notices

Event information may be updated as operational and technical arrangements are confirmed. The latest event regulations and notices published by Charles Michael Sports Hub will govern entry and participation. Any KTF or World Taekwondo sanction or endorsement will only be represented if formally confirmed.

Keep informed: Check the event page or contact Charles Michael Sports Hub for registration announcements and the final schedule.
